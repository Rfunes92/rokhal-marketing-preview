Services / the dispatch board

The website, plus the follow-up you don't have time for

Pick what you need. Each piece works on its own, and they work better together.

  1. Job 01Built with you

    Website

    A fast, mobile-first site that makes it easy for customers to call you or ask for a quote.

    • Tap-to-call buttons and a clear quote form
    • A page for each service and the towns you cover
    • Local SEO basics: titles, descriptions, structured data
  2. Job 02Runs automatically

    Missed-call text-back

    When you can't pick up, the caller gets a friendly text right away, so they're less likely to call the next shop.

    • Works on a ladder, under a sink or after hours
    • Written in your words, with your shop name
    • Replies land in one inbox on your phone
  3. Job 03Runs automatically

    Review requests

    After a job is done, customers get a simple text asking for a Google review, so you don't have to remember to ask.

    • Sent a set time after the job is marked complete
    • One tap to your Google review page
    • One gentle reminder if they miss it
  4. Job 04Runs automatically

    Quote follow-up

    Sent a quote and heard nothing? Automatic check-ins keep the conversation going until they say yes, or no.

    • Texts and emails on a schedule you choose
    • Stops as soon as the customer replies
    • Every open quote in one place

One place for it all

Every lead, review and quote. One screen.

Calls, texts, form leads and open quotes land in one simple dashboard you can check from your phone between jobs.

  • See who called, who texted back and who's waiting on a quote
  • See which finished jobs got a review request
  • Quote reminders run on their own and stop when the customer replies
